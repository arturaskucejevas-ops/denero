faq:
  - question: What is the difference between Microsoft Dynamics NAV and Dynamics 365 Business Central?
    answer: Business Central is the successor of Dynamics NAV – the same ERP
      family, but it runs in the cloud (or on-premises), has a modern browser
      interface, receives regular Microsoft updates and is customized through AL
      extensions instead of changing the standard code.
  - question: Can Dynamics NAV be upgraded to Business Central?
    answer: Yes. Data and existing customizations are migrated – custom C/AL code
      is rewritten as AL extensions, obsolete code is removed and the system is
      tested before go-live.
  - question: Can Business Central be integrated with other systems?
    answer: Yes. Business Central provides REST API, OData and SOAP interfaces, so
      it can be connected to e-shops, CRM, warehouse, banking (SEPA), EDI and
      other systems.

## Upgrading Dynamics NAV to Business Central

Older Dynamics NAV versions no longer receive Microsoft updates. We assess your current system, plan and carry out the move to Business Central (cloud or on-premises): we migrate data, rewrite customizations as AL extensions and keep your business running smoothly.

## Development & Customization

We build Business Central extensions (AL), maintain and improve C/AL code in Dynamics NAV, create RDLC and Word reports, automate manual processes and develop custom modules for your business needs.
