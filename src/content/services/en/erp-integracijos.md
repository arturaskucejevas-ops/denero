faq:
  - question: What is EDI integration?
    answer: EDI (Electronic Data Interchange) is the standardized electronic
      exchange of documents (orders, invoices, delivery notes) between companies,
      e.g. with retail chains. When EDI is integrated with ERP, documents are
      created automatically without manual entry.
  - question: Why do I need SEPA integration?
    answer: SEPA integration lets your ERP automatically generate bank payment
      files and import bank statements, saving time and reducing errors.
  - question: Which systems can be connected to Dynamics NAV or Business Central?
    answer: Practically any system with an API or file exchange (XML, JSON, CSV) –
      e-shops, CRM, warehouse, logistics, accounting, banking and EDI systems.
