We build and customize **websites**, **business portals** and **CRM systems** connected to your ERP and other systems.

## Websites & business portals

We build fast, secure, search-engine-optimized websites and customer or partner portals (ASP.NET Core, React, Microsoft Power Pages) where customers can place orders, submit requests or view documents.

## CRM systems

We implement and customize **Microsoft Dynamics 365 CRM** and **Dataverse** solutions: customer and contact management, sales pipeline, case and service management, automatic reminders and reporting.

## Integrations

We connect websites, portals and CRM with Dynamics NAV / Business Central, email, payment and other external systems so all data is in one place.
