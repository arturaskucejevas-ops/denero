**DENERO Apskaita** is accounting software that runs in your browser at [apskaita.denero.eu](https://apskaita.denero.eu). It is built for small, growing and larger companies as well as accounting firms.

## Modules

* Purchases and sales
* Inventory
* Fixed assets
* Cash desk and advance reports
* Payment batches and currencies
* Tax reports and i.VAZ
* WooCommerce, Shopify, PrestaShop, Paysera, Montonio integrations and API
* Add-on modules: contracts, HR and payroll, manufacturing

## Plans (prices excl. VAT)

* **Start** – €5 / month or €55 / year, 3 users
* **Business** – €10 / month or €110 / year, 5 users
* **Business+** – €18 / month or €198 / year, 10 users
* **Custom** – for accounting firms and larger companies, on request
