**DENERO PBI** is a set of 25 ready-made **Microsoft Power BI** management and finance reports built for **Dynamics 365 Business Central** and **Dynamics NAV** users. Managers, finance and sales teams see profit, balance sheet, cash flow, budget execution, receivables, sales, inventory and HR metrics in one place – without manual exports to Excel.

Using a different accounting or ERP system? **Data can also be connected from other systems** – via SQL database, views, API or files.

**Price – €50 + VAT per year for all reports.**
